Privacy

Your guest profile and local chat/focus history are stored in your browser. When you send a Chat or Classroom question, that text is sent to the site’s Cloudflare Pages Function so the AI tutor can respond. Voice text may be sent to the configured server voice provider, or spoken locally by your browser if the server voice is unavailable.

Do not put passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information into study questions.
