QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Is Turtle Study Buddy free?

Yes. The current live preview is free. There is no paid plan or checkout.

Where is my profile saved?

Your guest nickname, age band, subject and local history stay in this browser. Questions sent to the tutor are sent to the site’s Cloudflare AI function to generate replies.

Does Turtle just give answers?

The tutor is instructed to explain, demonstrate and give a short practice step instead of pretending learning is only about copying an answer.

Does Voice use a microphone?

No. Voice currently reads text aloud. It does not capture or store microphone audio.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes. The home and learner pages adapt to smaller screens.
