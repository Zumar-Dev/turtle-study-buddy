FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Simple answers about the live free preview.

Is this a real AI tutor?

Yes. Chat and Classroom call the site’s existing Cloudflare Workers AI function for live responses.

Is it free?

Yes. The current site is free for now. There is no paid plan or checkout.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Start as guest and create a local nickname/profile. That profile stays in your browser.

How does voice work?

The site tries a server-generated voice first. If that is unavailable, it automatically uses a voice built into your browser.

Does Turtle remember personal facts?

The tutor is instructed not to claim you said something unless it appears in the visible conversation.
