FAQ

Questions about Turtle Study Buddy

Short answers about how the study experience works.

Is Turtle just an answer generator?

No. Turtle is designed to explain, demonstrate, give practice, offer hints and check understanding instead of simply handing over an answer.

Who is it for?

The current experience is designed for learners aged 10 and up, with age-band adaptation.

What is Classroom?

Classroom is the structured teaching mode. Your question stays on the board while Turtle works through explanation, example and practice.

How does the talking Turtle work?

The voice runs with a free in-browser neural text-to-speech engine. Its phoneme/viseme timing drives four mouth poses, blinking and subtle body animation.

Does a guest need an email or password?

No. Guest mode saves only a nickname and study preferences in that browser.

Is it free?

Yes. The preview is free for now and does not collect payment details.
